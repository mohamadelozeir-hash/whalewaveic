About WhaleWave IC

Our Expertise

At WhaleWave IC, we provide reliable and precise IC design and layout services with deep expertise in analog, RF, and mixed-signal domains.

We specialize in Digital RF, high-speed interfaces, RF/MMW transceivers, high-performance and high-voltage analog/power ICs, electrical interfaces for photonic circuits, and more.

Way of Intervention

We operate flexibly to integrate seamlessly with your project. Our engineers work on our own workstations while accessing your EDA software, licenses, and cloud/compute resources remotely.

Why Work With Us

  • Scalable resources — Easily increase the number of engineers as needed
  • Flexible deployment — Workstations provided by us, software & compute by the client
  • Seamless collaboration — Fully integrated with client teams and workflows
  • Secure & controlled — Accountable, tracked, and reliable workflows. The client has control.
  • Reduced time-to-market — Optimized design process and efficient resource use

From Concept to Silicon

End-to-End IC Development

  • System Study: Assess system-level requirements, constraints, and trade-offs
  • Architectural Analysis: Validate architecture choices against target KPIs
  • Requirements Definition: Define specifications at both top level and IP-block level
  • Full-Custom Schematic & Layout: Deliver optimized implementations tailored to your targets
  • IO / ESD & EMIR / Parasitic Analysis: Ensure robustness, reliability, and compliance
  • LVS, DRC & ERC Checks: Verify physical and electrical integrity before tape-out
  • Verification & Simulation: Run PVT corners and Monte Carlo analyses
  • Co-Simulation: Combine die and package models for accurate performance prediction
  • Test Planning: Prepare wafer-test and final-test strategies
  • Laboratory Support: Support measurement, debugging, and characterization activities
